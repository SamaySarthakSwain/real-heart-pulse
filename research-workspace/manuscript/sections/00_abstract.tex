\begin{abstract}
Cardiovascular diseases remain the predominant cause of mortality worldwide, claiming 17.9 million lives each year. Conventional clinical monitoring depends on episodic 12-lead electrocardiography (ECG) or cumbersome Holter units, both of which suffer from poor ambulatory compliance and miss transient arrhythmogenic episodes. This paper introduces \textit{Ear to Heart}, an autonomous auricular physiological telemetry and deep multimodal diagnostic system. By utilizing the electrophysiological and hemodynamic properties of the external ear canal and concha, the system integrates a low-noise dry-contact ECG front-end (AD8232) in a tragus-earlobe-canal montage, dual-wavelength reflective photoplethysmography (MAX30102), and an ESP32 edge microcontroller executing BLE 5.0 telemetry at 250\,Hz. To solve the clinical failure mode of pseudo-12-lead channel cloning (which distorts cardiac vector geometry and induces diagnostic collapse in chamber enlargement), we formulate a native single-lead deep architecture combining a 1D Squeeze-and-Excitation Residual Network with Bidirectional Gated Recurrent Units and Temporal Multi-Head Attention. Evaluated across the benchmark PhysioNet MIT-BIH Arrhythmia database using an inter-patient partition (51,002 train beats and 47,432 unseen test beats), the model attains 98.02\% accuracy on Ventricular Ectopic Beat (PVC) detection, with 98.35\% ROC-AUC and 99.08\% specificity. Across all 21,799 authentic 10-second Lead-II recordings from the PhysioNet PTB-XL database, our native single-lead network attains 83.60\% Macro ROC-AUC, completely resolving the Hypertrophy sensitivity collapse observed under cloned inputs. In parallel, a multimodal cross-attention fusion model achieves 98.20\% accuracy and 1.0000 ROC-AUC. Heterogeneous stacking ensembles evaluated across 113,449 patient records reach 95.76\% ROC-AUC on heart failure cohorts and 80.60\% on cardiovascular registries. The diagnostic pipeline integrates 1D Grad-CAM for temporal feature attribution and Inductive Split-Conformal Prediction with 95.0\% distribution-free coverage guarantees. This system provides a verified, interpretable, hospital-grade foundation for continuous ambulatory cardiac telemetry.
\end{abstract}
